2. DETAILED MODULE WIRING SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 ONBOARD LED (ACTUATOR STATE INDICATOR)
    - Pin: ESP32 GPIO 2 (Internal connection on standard DevKit boards)
    - Logic: HIGH (3.3V) = LED ON, LOW (0V) = LED OFF

2.2 L298N DUAL DC MOTOR DRIVER MODULE
    IMPORTANT: Remove the black jumpers from the ENA and ENB pins on the L298N
    board so the ESP32 can send analog PWM signals for speed regulation.

    [ESP32 Connection Pinout]
    - ENA  --> ESP32 GPIO 14  (Motor A Speed Control - 5 kHz PWM)
    - IN1  --> ESP32 GPIO 26  (Motor A Direction Pin 1)
    - IN2  --> ESP32 GPIO 27  (Motor A Direction Pin 2)
    - ENB  --> ESP32 GPIO 13  (Motor B Speed Control - 5 kHz PWM)
    - IN3  --> ESP32 GPIO 32  (Motor B Direction Pin 1)
    - IN4  --> ESP32 GPIO 33  (Motor B Direction Pin 2)

    [Motor & Power Connections]
    - OUT1 & OUT2 Terminals --> DC Motor A (Left Motor)
    - OUT3 & OUT4 Terminals --> DC Motor B (Right Motor)
    - 12V Power Screw Terminal --> External Battery Positive (+7V to +12V DC)
    - GND Screw Terminal       --> External Battery Negative (-) AND ESP32 GND (COMMON GROUND)
    - 5V Screw Terminal        --> Can connect to ESP32 VIN/5V pin if jumper is enabled.

    [Motor Control Truth Table]
    +----------+----------+-------------------+
    | IN1 / IN3| IN2 / IN4| MOTOR BEHAVIOR    |
    +----------+----------+-------------------+
    |   HIGH   |   LOW    | Forward Direction |
    |   LOW    |   HIGH   | Backward Direction|
    |   LOW    |   LOW    | Stop (Brake)      |
    |   HIGH   |   HIGH   | Stop (Brake)      |
    +----------+----------+-------------------+

2.3 SERVO MOTOR (SG90 / MG996R)
    - Signal (Yellow / Orange Wire) --> ESP32 GPIO 18 (50 Hz, 16-bit LEDC PWM)
    - VCC Power (Red Wire)          --> External 5V Power Supply or L298N 5V Out
    - Ground (Brown / Black Wire)   --> ESP32 GND (Common System Ground)

2.4 ESP32-CAM MODULE (STANDALONE VISION MODULE)
    - VCC / 5V Pin                  --> 5V Power Supply (1A+ recommended)
    - GND Pin                       --> Common System Ground
    - Onboard Flash LED             --> GPIO 4 (Controlled via HTTP GET on Port 80)
    - Stream WebServer              --> Broadcasts live MJPEG stream on Port 81 (:81/stream)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CRITICAL WIRING RULES & CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[✓] COMMON GROUND MANDATORY: All grounds (ESP32 GND, L298N GND, Servo GND, 
    ESP32-CAM GND, and Battery (-)) MUST be connected together. Without a 
    common ground reference, motor PWM signals will fluctuate unpredictably.

[✓] SEPARATE MOTOR POWER: Do NOT power DC motors or heavy servos directly from 
    the ESP32 3.3V pin. Motors cause voltage spikes that will trigger brownout 
    resets on the ESP32.

[✓] L298N JUMPERS: Ensure the ENA and ENB jumpers are REMOVED to allow speed 
    modulation via GPIO 14 and GPIO 13.
